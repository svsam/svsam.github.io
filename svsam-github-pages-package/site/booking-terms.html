Consumer booking boundaries

This prototype cannot reserve accommodation.

It organises search criteria locally and illustrates where approved external providers could appear later.

Related pages Consumer prototype Privacy Affiliate disclosure

What the prototype does not do

  • It does not search live inventory or guarantee a property, room, price, discount, ranking or availability.
  • It does not accept payment, issue confirmation, hold a reservation or create a contract for accommodation.
  • It does not amend, cancel or refund bookings and does not handle property, access, safety or emergency support.
  • It is not evidence of a partnership with a named platform.

Future referral phase

If approved partner links are later enabled, visitors will leave SvS to verify and complete the transaction with the clearly named provider. That provider's current property information, total price, payment, cancellation, privacy and support terms will apply.

Visitors must check accessibility requirements, rules, location, fees, taxes, identity requirements and cancellation conditions directly with the provider before paying.

Direct booking remains out of scope

On-site checkout will not be enabled merely because a provider publishes API documentation. It requires express partner permission, reviewed consumer terms, secure server-side systems, payment and fraud controls, privacy/data governance, insurance, complaints and support arrangements, monitoring and a revised capacity decision.

Urgent accommodation problem?

Contact the property or the platform shown on your real booking confirmation. Use the appropriate emergency service where safety is at risk. The SvS prototype is not monitored as an emergency or booking-support channel.
