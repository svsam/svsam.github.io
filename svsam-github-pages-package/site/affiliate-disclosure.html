Current position

  • SvS / SvSam is not currently claiming to be affiliated with, endorsed by, authorised by or acting as agent for Airbnb, Booking.com or another accommodation platform.
  • No commission is currently earned from the disabled provider cards.
  • No provider logo, property image, review, price, availability or inventory feed is used.
  • Provider names identify possible future external destinations only; the relevant trade marks belong to their owners.

If an affiliate arrangement is approved

Any paid or commission-generating placement will be clearly identified before the visitor interacts with it. The page will explain that SvS may receive commission, identify the booking provider and separate sponsored placement from any independent ordering.

Only programme-approved links, wording and brand materials will be used. Programme documentation or an application does not itself prove approval.

Who owns the booking

During any referral or search-and-redirect phase, the named external provider will show the final availability, total price, taxes/fees, property terms and cancellation conditions. Checkout, confirmation, payment, amendments, refunds and booking support will take place under that provider's terms.
