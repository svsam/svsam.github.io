75-second walkthrough

What happens after you say yes?

Read this short version, then open the synthetic report to see the end result.

Open the sample report ↗

First, we choose one recurring client-reporting workflow—not your entire reporting estate. Together we record the current steps, time spent, source totals, deadlines, and the definition of an acceptable output.

Next, we map up to the agreed number of aggregate data sources in accounts you own. The build includes documented transformations, reconciliation checks, anomaly flags, and a branded report based on your existing client experience.

Then, we run it through a 30-day proof period. AI may organise inputs and draft commentary, but every numerical claim must trace to source data. A human reviews the transformations, exceptions, privacy, and final draft.

Finally, we compare the new run with your baseline. A successful pilot means at least 50% less reporting effort, no material reconciliation discrepancy, and delivery on time. If it works, the workflow moves into a managed monthly service; if it does not, the gap is documented rather than hidden.

Common questions

Useful detail, before a call.

The diagnostic is the best first step. A 20-minute call is offered only where the workflow appears suitable.

Are you replacing our reporting platform?

No. The service connects a defined workflow around the tools and client-owned systems you already approve. It may produce a report in an agreed format, but the offer is managed operations—not another general-purpose dashboard product.

Will AI send commentary to our clients?

No. AI may prepare a draft using approved inputs, with facts, inferences, and unknowns separated. A human reviews it, and your agency retains approval over anything sent to a client.

What data can the workflow use?

The first-year scope is aggregate marketing performance data. Raw CRM records, credentials, special-category data, and unnecessary personal data are excluded. Production sources and subprocessors are agreed before access.

What happens if the pilot misses the proof criteria?

The baseline comparison and gaps are documented. Continuing work is not assumed. Commercial terms, acceptance, liability, termination, data handling, and IP are set out in the written agreement before the pilot starts.

Who owns the output and the underlying tools?

Your agency retains its data and branded deliverables. Reusable code and templates remain provider background IP, licensed for the agreed client use. Exact rights are confirmed in the written agreement.

How much agency time is needed?

We need a workflow owner, access to approved aggregate sources, a baseline run, and timely review decisions. The diagnostic helps estimate fit before either side commits to a call or pilot.
