Who operates this site

The legal operator is Sam Seifi, sole trader, using the temporary working name SvS, expanded as SvSam.

The public privacy mailbox and correspondence route are intentionally omitted from this public-source prototype while mailbox MFA, business suitability and address publication remain unapproved.

The working name and contact presentation remain subject to clearance. This page must be completed and reviewed before the site becomes public or begins collecting consumer information.

What happens now

Consumer stay-search prototype

Destination, dates, guest count and accommodation type are processed in your browser only to build a demonstration summary. They are not transmitted, saved to local storage, used for profiling or sent to a booking platform.

Agency diagnostic

The agency diagnostic is also prepared locally. It leaves the browser only if the visitor deliberately opens and sends an email through their own email application.

Cookies, analytics and hosting

The prototype sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising pixels, embedded booking widgets or third-party scripts. GitHub Pages serves the website and may process ordinary connection and security information under GitHub's own terms and privacy arrangements.

Do not enter credentials, payment information, identity documents, booking references, guest details, health/accessibility information, property access codes or other sensitive information anywhere on this prototype.

External booking providers

No live partner connection exists. If a future page links to an external provider, that provider will operate its own website and apply its own privacy notice, cookies and booking terms. SvS will identify the destination before a visitor leaves this site.

SvS will not receive booking, payment or traveller data merely because a visitor follows a link unless a later approved integration explicitly says otherwise and this notice is updated first.

Before anything changes

Affiliate tracking, provider APIs, server logs, analytics, accounts, saved trips, contact forms, newsletters and direct booking are disabled. Before enabling any of them, the operator must document the purpose, lawful basis, data recipients, international transfers, retention, security, cookie/consent requirements and controller/processor roles.

API tokens and confidential partner data will never be placed in GitHub Pages or browser JavaScript. Any approved integration must use a separate secured server-side service and a revised privacy notice.

Questions, requests or complaints

A monitored privacy contact and complaints route will be published before launch. Do not use this demonstration to submit a rights request or include sensitive information.

This pre-launch page is operational information, not a substitute for final legal review.
